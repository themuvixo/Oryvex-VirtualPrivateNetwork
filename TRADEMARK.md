# Name and branding

"OryvexCore", the OryvexCore logo (`branding/`) and app icons are the identity of this project.

OryvexCore is an independent fork of the upstream **Aether** project. Aether's trademark policy asks derivative projects to use a different name and branding, which is why this fork is renamed and carries its own logo. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the upstream Aether project.
