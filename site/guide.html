How to use

Three taps to connected.

Install, pick a route, connect. The defaults are good; change them only when your network pushes back.

  1. Install. Android: the APK for your phone (arm64-v8a for most). Windows: unzip and run OryvexVPN.exe.
  2. Pick a route. Leave it on Auto, or choose Tor for the Tor network with no tunnel underneath.
  3. Choose a Tor exit (Tor route): Auto, Fastest, or a Country such as Germany or Netherlands.
  4. Connect. The orb shows each stage live; it turns Connected only after HTTPS returns 200 through the tunnel.
Proxy mode

Point any app at it.

The core serves a SOCKS5 proxy. Use socks5h so DNS goes through the tunnel too.

socks5h://127.0.0.1:1819

Check it works:

curl -x socks5h://127.0.0.1:1819 https://www.cloudflare.com/cdn-cgi/trace

Seeing warp=on means your traffic is passing through the tunnel.

When it won't connect

Turn it up one step at a time.

Nothing connects

Switch MASQUE to HTTP/2. If that handshake is blocked too, add ClientHello fragmentation. Then try WireGuard or classic Gool.

Connects, then drops

Take the obfuscation profile one step heavier: gfw for MASQUE, aggressive for WireGuard.

Scan is slow

Use the turbo scan, and say yes to reconnecting to your last working gateway.

Tor is blocked

Bridges are fetched for you. Or run Tor through the tunnel. Tor bridges
